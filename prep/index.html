Before you walk in

Wording that keeps the conversation on the work, not on a word.

  • Say "PTO scheduling."Not "holiday time off."
  • Say "the specialist program is shelved."Not "paused" or "on hold."
  • Say "new hires can buy boots on the Walmart account."Not "boots are on hand."
  • Bring questions, not answers.Leave fixes, owners, and effort to them. Guessing could offend.
  • Say "it came up in 6 of 8 sessions."Never a name, a session, or a department.
  • Open with what people value.Coworkers came up in 7 of 8 sessions. Then what's already changed.
